The screen should fit the counter

A POS can be modern without becoming decorative.

Large touch targets, readable totals, clear states, and a predictable path matter more than animation for animation’s sake. The visual design should help a cashier move with confidence at 5:30 PM when the line is six people deep.

Streamline POS keeps the interface focused on what the employee needs now, while customer and product information stay available when they matter.
