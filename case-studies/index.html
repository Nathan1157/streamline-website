Case studies

Start with the operational problem. Then look at the software.

The strongest software stories are not feature lists. They are examples of friction removed from a real business process.

Venue operations

From handwritten waitlist to a full guest-flow operating system.

The problem: staff needed one place for waitlists, events, lanes/resources, arrivals, and guest-facing screens without adding unnecessary complexity.

The solution: a purpose-built desktop system with waitlist management, event reservations, lane/resource scheduling, kiosk flows, digital signage, arrival states, payment indicators, and locked staff/guest modes.

Representative project narrative. Final production case study can be updated with client-approved names, screenshots, and measured outcomes.

Sales & operations

A single internal system from lead intake to completed installation.

The problem: sales, scheduling, payments, contracts, installation, complaints, payroll, and management oversight can become fragmented when each step lives in a separate tool.

The solution: an internal operating system that connects lead assignment, consultation scheduling, quoting, pricing approval, payment, contracts, install scheduling, job completion, complaints, and payroll.

Representative internal product case study. Replace with finalized launch metrics when available.
