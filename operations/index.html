Streamline Operations

A central operating workspace for the work that falls between departments.

Streamline Operations is for recurring business workflows that do not belong in a spreadsheet, inbox, or pile of notes. It creates a controlled home for tasks, handoffs, recurring procedures, management visibility, and day-to-day operational follow-through.
