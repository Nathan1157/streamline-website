How Streamline works

Start with the operation. Choose the smallest system that genuinely solves it.

The goal is not to sell you the biggest software package. It is to understand the workflow, identify the fit, and keep the ownership model straightforward.

01

Show us the work

Walk through what happens today: who touches it, where information moves, and where things break down.

02

Match the fit

We determine whether an industry system, a business-wide tool, a combination, or no Streamline product is the right answer.

03

Set it up around the business

Configure the product around the actual roles and workflow instead of forcing the team to imitate a demo account.

04

Own what you bought

The core software is sold as a one-time purchase. Ongoing charges are reserved for optional services that create real ongoing infrastructure costs.

A deliberate pricing philosophy

A software company should have to keep earning recurring revenue—not just lock access behind it.
